# Residual GED Target-Clean Retrospective Replication — Research Start

Date: 2026-09-29

The replication remains motivated by correction-acceptability research: post-correction grammaticality/context signals can distinguish locally plausible edits from complete repairs. Arabic GEC ensemble and edit-voting literature also shows that agreement alone does not eliminate correlated errors. The test therefore keeps the GED target-clean signal isolated and frozen, rather than combining post-hoc features.

This stage is deliberately retrospective and falsification-oriented: failure closes the hypothesis without spending a new disjoint slice.
