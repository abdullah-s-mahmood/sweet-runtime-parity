PROPOSED METAMORPHIC TRACK

Use authentic academic/scientific source text and deterministic mutations.

PASS-preserving examples:
- sentence split/merge preserving propositions
- safe clause reorder
- frozen safe synonym substitution
- format-only changes
- citation-style change preserving ownership

REJECT-guaranteed examples:
- owner/value swap
- group-label swap
- explicit negation flip
- unsupported MAY/CAN -> IS strengthening
- association -> causation
- citation-owner swap
- equation coefficient/variable swap
- denominator/baseline change
- temporal-scope change
- deletion of critical qualifier

REVIEW:
only transformations with a formally preregistered information-loss condition.
Do not assume generic truncation means REVIEW.

- recent NLP/LLM work applies MT at scale;
- MT can reveal robustness defects not captured by static accuracy.

1. Construct mismatch:

2. Hidden benchmark duplication:

3. Public benchmark familiarity:

5. Domain skew:
many resources are biomedical/news/NLP, not all academic domains.

6. REVIEW undercoverage:
external datasets often emphasize binary factuality.

7. Full-document gap:
many tracks operate at sentence/claim/abstract level.

8. Prevalence mismatch:
benchmark class balance cannot be interpreted as deployment prevalence.
