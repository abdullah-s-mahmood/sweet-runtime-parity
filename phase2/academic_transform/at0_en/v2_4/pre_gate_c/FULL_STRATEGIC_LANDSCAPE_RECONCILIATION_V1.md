`ORIGINAL DOCUMENT + TASK CONTRACT -> ANCHORED REPRESENTATION / OBLIGATIONS -> PROPOSED EDIT -> INDEPENDENT EXTRACTION + COVERAGE AUDIT -> SUPPORT + PRESERVATION + STRUCTURAL CHECKS -> DECISION -> BOUNDED REPAIR -> DEPENDENCY/GLOBAL RE-VERIFICATION -> COMMIT OR ROLLBACK`

## 6. Failure modes added explicitly to ACAD_PASS roadmap

### Shared extraction blindness
Source and candidate extraction can miss the same protected fact/relation.

Required future mitigation:
coverage audit independent of mutual alignment.

### Benchmark packaging leakage
Ordering, IDs, sibling pairs, generator labels or file structure may leak class.

Mitigation:
neutral immutable IDs; isolated inference artifacts; shuffled/order-independent evaluation where applicable.

### Multiplicity collapse
Repeated measurements/groups/visits may be merged into one assertion.

Mitigation:
occurrence identity + cardinality.

### Context laundering
Verifier may use surrounding/source/external context to “repair” a candidate omission during judgment.

Mitigation:
separate interpretation context from candidate-preserved content.

### Evidence cherry-picking
One supporting fragment may mask contradictory subgroup/condition evidence.

Mitigation:
conflict-aware evidence sets and scope tracking.

### Stale verification
A table/equation/reference changes while old textual PASS evidence remains cached.

Mitigation:
dependency/version invalidation.

### Compensatory repair
Repair improves factuality by deleting a required difficult fact.

Mitigation:
fixed obligation ledger checked after every repair.

### Hidden document layers
Tracked changes/comments/footnotes/text boxes/figures may differ from verified plain text.

Mitigation:
delivery artifact verification and native-object checks.

### Scientific-state confusion
Proposed vs observed; nonsignificance vs equivalence; association vs causation.

Mitigation:
state/evidential-strength obligations.

### Authoritative-source drift
Retraction/correction/version status changes after evidence cache.

Mitigation:
source authority/version/timestamp binding; separate fidelity from open-world truth.
