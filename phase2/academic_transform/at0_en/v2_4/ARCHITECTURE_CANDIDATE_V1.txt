AT0-EN V2.4 Architecture Candidate V1

Status: PRE-IMPLEMENTATION / CONSULTATION CANDIDATE.

V2.3 failed the frozen second holdout: 9/24 adversarial escapes (37.5%) and 3/12 safe automatic acceptance (25%). Dominant unsafe failure: relation rebinding under lexical preservation. Dominant usability failure: paraphrase brittleness.

Selected design: Hybrid Scientific Assertion Frame + Assertion Relation Graph.

Scientific Assertion Frame fields:
assertion id; exact source provenance; discourse role; assertion type; subject; normalized predicate; object; value; comparator; direction; unit; arguments; conditions; time; population; baseline; modality; evidential strength; polarity; causality class; scope; exclusions; citation refs; equation refs; symbol bindings; extraction confidence/status; unresolved slots.

Assertion Relation Graph nodes:
assertions, entities, quantities, units, times, populations, conditions, metrics, citations, equations/symbols and methods.

Verification:
Lane A deterministic invariants for exact quantities, units, symbols, equations, citations, counts, timestamps, seeds and identifiers.
Lane B semantic relation verification for paraphrase, role alignment, scope, modality, evidential strength, causality/association, negation scope and relation rebinding.
Semantic outputs: MATCH / MISMATCH / UNCERTAIN. Critical UNCERTAIN -> REVIEW.

Source and candidate are extracted independently. Alignment classifies source assertions as PRESERVED / ALTERED / OMITTED / UNCERTAIN and candidate assertions as ALIGNED_EXISTING / NEW_INFORMATION / CONTRADICTORY / UNCERTAIN.

Automatic PASS requires no deterministic contradiction, no material omission, no unsupported new information, all critical relation matches, no critical uncertainty, and correct ownership of deterministic anchors.

Do not patch against individual consumed V2.3 holdout IDs. A new untouched holdout is allowed only after V2.4 is frozen.

Before implementation, source extraction itself must be evaluated for coverage, atomicity, decontextualization, role binding, anchor ownership, modality/polarity, citation/equation ownership and abstention quality.

Research basis: Claimify (ACL 2025), Optimizing Decomposition (ACL 2025), DnDScore (EMNLP 2025), SciEvent (EMNLP 2025), Verify-in-the-Graph (NAACL 2025), factuality stress testing (ACL 2026), Verify with Caution (ACL Findings 2025).

Recommendation: proceed with this architecture only after higher-model consultation. No implementation authorized yet.
