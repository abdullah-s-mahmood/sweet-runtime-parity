## B. Critical new issue — second-order stacking leakage

Original draft Section 10 proposed 5-fold head CV over the same cross-fitted candidate bank.

That is not sufficiently isolated for an unbiased head comparison.

Example with OOF folds A/B/C/D/E:
- candidate/features for A are generated by an ancestor trained on B+C+D+E.
- candidate/features for B are generated by an ancestor trained on A+C+D+E.
- if a head is evaluated on A but trained on B/C/D/E OOF rows, portions of its training feature population were produced by ancestors whose supervised training INCLUDED A.

Thus A's labels/data can influence the feature-generation models that generated the meta-training rows used to fit the head tested on A.

This is not direct row leakage and does not invalidate the OOF bank as a training resource. It **does invalidate treating ordinary CV over that bank as clean performance estimation for stacked model selection**.

### Required correction

R4.4 must be staged.

### R44-A — OOF UPSTREAM BANK ONLY
Use DESIGN=256 only.
Generate 5-fold OOF:
- B proposals;
- constrained BIO violations;
- Boundary scalar support;
- frozen-base context;
- exact gold/NONE targets;
- type-correction cases;
- goldless proposal cases;
- error taxonomy.

Freeze the complete candidate bank and diagnostics.

STOP.

No J0/J1 fitting in R44-A.

### R44-B — HEAD SELECTION
Design only after R44-A error distribution is known, but without using VERIFY_INTERNAL.

Two acceptable leakage-safe options:

**Option B1 — Nested outer/inner document CV (scientifically strongest)**
For each outer DESIGN evaluation fold:
- hold outer fold completely untouched by every upstream model used to construct head-training features;
- inside outer-train, generate inner-OOF upstream features/candidates for meta-training;
- train head on those inner-OOF candidates;
- train one upstream model on full outer-train and apply to outer-eval;
- evaluate head on outer-eval.
Aggregate every outer prediction once.

This is computationally expensive but clean.

**Option B2 — Fixed disjoint stack-development partition (cheaper)**
Prospectively split DESIGN into:
- UPSTREAM_META_TRAIN;
- HEAD_SELECT.
Generate the head training bank entirely from OOF inside UPSTREAM_META_TRAIN.
Train/refit upstream only on UPSTREAM_META_TRAIN and apply once to HEAD_SELECT.
Use HEAD_SELECT once for J0/J1/threshold selection.
VERIFY_INTERNAL remains unopened.

B2 sacrifices training data but has a simpler leakage argument.

Do NOT decide B1 vs B2 from exposed SELECT or VERIFY results.

## C. Final-refit distribution shift

A head trained on OOF upstream errors may face a different distribution when the final B/Boundary ancestors are trained on all DESIGN.

The original design only proposed an unlabeled shift alert. That is insufficient as the sole safeguard.

Preferred confirmation choices:
1. preserve the cross-fitted/ensemble architecture at confirmation time; or
2. use leakage-safe nested evaluation to estimate the refit transition; or
3. predefine a fold-model assignment/ensemble for VERIFY so its upstream training fraction mirrors meta-training.

Do not train a head on OOF failures then silently deploy it behind an in-sample/perfect full-refit candidate generator without measuring candidate-distribution transport.

## D. Unknown negative imbalance must be measured before head loss is frozen

The number and composition of real OOF B errors is currently unknown.

Therefore the primary head loss/sampling policy should NOT be irrevocably frozen before R44-A.

At R44-A freeze, report:
- total proposals;
- exact-coordinate correct;
- exact-type correct;
- wrong-type exact-coordinate;
- same-type wrong-boundary;
- cross-type wrong-boundary;
- no-overlap;
- candidates in goldless examples;
- NONE:P:I:C:O ratio;
- high-confidence FP distribution;
- class C support.

Then prospectively freeze R44-B:
- ordinary CE if imbalance is moderate;
- otherwise one predeclared imbalance remedy such as capped deterministic hard-negative sampling, focal/IoU-aware loss, or two-stage existence/type separation.

No method should be selected by trying several on the future HEAD_SELECT labels.

## E. Candidate correction scope

Joint target assignment by exact coordinates, regardless of B proposed type, is a strong improvement because it allows type correction.

Still:
- wrong-boundary candidates become NONE and cannot be repaired;
- missed candidates remain unrecoverable;
- boundary repair is intentionally deferred.

This is correct for R44-A/B because candidate ceiling in R4.3 exceeded the current recall floor. Do not introduce repair until corrected verifier precision is measured.

## F. Section feature audit

Original EBM-NLPmod pipeline focuses on Title/Methods and source TRAIN visibly contains section markers.

Section metadata is permitted only if derived from raw text, not labels.

Preflight must prove:
- deterministic TITLE/METHODS propagation;
- UNKNOWN rate;
- no Results/Conclusion content silently included under METHODS because a marker is missing;
- section feature is disabled if provenance cannot be reconstructed reliably.

Do not use a guessed section label.

## G. Calibration

R4.3 common threshold combined incomparable probabilities from multiple models.

R4.4 joint 5-way softmax is structurally cleaner, but neural probabilities are not automatically calibrated.

R44-B must separate:
- representation/head fitting;
- calibration;
- architecture/threshold selection.

Use a calibration subset/fold unseen by the fitted head if temperature/isotonic calibration is used.
Brier/ECE/risk-coverage are diagnostics unless calibration is prospectively part of the model.
