## Disconfirming evidence and alternatives

| Evidence | Consequence for this acquisition |

| [FinePICO, 2025](https://penglab.weill.cornell.edu/publications/semi-supervised-learning-small-annotated-data-and-large-unlabeled-data-fine-grained), with [public methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19346v1), uses older public corpora and a different fine-grained schema; its methods describe sentence-level splitting in PICO-Corpus. | Useful evidence about annotation inconsistency and data efficiency, not an independent four-class replacement benchmark or evidence that another DESIGN adaptation is authorized. |

| [Grounded biomedical LLM annotation, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-35492-8) reports 91.3% precision of suggestions, assessed with authors of six papers. | Suggestion precision is not exhaustive exact-boundary P/I/C/O gold quality or recall. It does not justify replacing independent annotation. |
| [Contamination study, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06059), [cross-language contamination, 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.990/), and [MMLU-CF, 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.656/) | Protect text as well as labels; literal matching alone cannot exclude paraphrases, translations or related reports. Closed evaluation custody is necessary even for publicly available source articles. |
| [Anglin, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26422) examines uncertainty with nested texts and small/high-performing samples. | Entity counts are not independent sample counts. Do not apply a binomial interval to every repeated span or treat a naive bootstrap as an exact guarantee. |

Competing explanations remain: weak candidate-validity ranking; boundary/role ambiguity in the annotation construct; development selection; and temporal/domain shift. Fresh data repairs the independence of future evidence and exposes these possibilities; it does not guarantee better model performance. Reusing a public corpus is cheaper but does not currently establish the required non-exposure and schema compatibility. C-enrichment increases class support but changes prevalence and difficulty. AI annotation reduces cost but introduces anchoring and correlated errors. The smallest defensible scientific intervention is a new independently annotated, prospectively separated corpus, without another DESIGN model.

## Frozen acquisition specification — all 40 decisions

**1. Exact PubMed retrieval query.** Use the following literal query, with no hidden UI filters:
```text
(
  "randomized controlled trial"[Publication Type]
  OR "controlled clinical trial"[Publication Type]
  OR randomized[Title/Abstract]
  OR randomised[Title/Abstract]
  OR randomly[Title/Abstract]
  OR placebo[Title/Abstract]
)
AND ("2026/01/01"[Date - Publication] : "2026/09/30"[Date - Publication])
AND english[Language]
AND hasabstract
NOT (animals[MeSH Terms] NOT humans[MeSH Terms])
```
This is a reproducible candidate frame followed by human eligibility screening, not a claim of exhaustive retrieval of every RCT worldwide. Do not require positive Humans indexing, MEDLINE membership, open access, a named disease, explicit C wording or a positive result. [PubMed documents indexing-dependent filters and the distinction between publication and processing dates](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/help/).

**2. Temporal window.** Inclusive 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-30 for the earliest public version of the primary results report, including a matching preprint. Reject a 2026 print issue whose primary results text appeared before 2026. Earlier registration/protocol existence alone does not disqualify a genuinely new results report; prior ACAD_PASS exposure to that trial family does.

**3. Date field.** Retrieval uses Publication Date [dp]. Eligibility uses a separately recorded EARLIEST_PUBLIC_RESULTS_DATE derived from electronic publication, print publication and identified preprint/conference-result predecessors. Entry/indexing dates cannot establish freshness. Save every contributing field and source. For incomplete dates, require the entire possible date interval to lie within the window; otherwise seek documentary resolution before allocation or exclude as DATE_UNRESOLVED. Do not treat imputed January 1 as a verified day.

**4. Retrieval timestamp.** The custodian records a UTC start immediately before the first authorized ESearch and a UTC time for every response. Complete one acquisition session within 24 hours; archive raw queries, QueryTranslation, warnings, total counts, complete PMID lists, raw XML, pagination and hashes. Retrieve the entire result set, not Best Match top-k. If a service return cap is encountered, partition publication dates deterministically into months, then days as necessary, union PMIDs and reconcile counts; cap-truncated output is invalid. Save the original parent result count and every partition response. A session that cannot be completed consistently is an unconsumed acquisition failure requiring a documented restart before screening or annotation. Later replays use the archived frame, not a new live search. The population claim is limited to eligible records discoverable in this dated frame.

**5. RCT eligibility.** English-language primary journal report, human prospective random allocation to at least two intervention strategies, health-related outcome results in the abstract. Include individual/cluster, crossover, factorial, multi-arm, pragmatic, adaptive, noninferiority and equivalence designs; include pilot trials when they report randomized health outcomes. Include pharmacologic and nonpharmacologic interventions. Two independent screeners decide; a third resolves disagreements. Scope identification and eligibility do not depend on model predictions or span counts.

**6. Exclusions.** Nonrandom/quasi-random/Mendelian-randomization studies; observational, animal/in-vitro studies; protocols; feasibility-only reports without randomized health outcomes; reviews/editorials; secondary, subgroup, post-hoc, economic-only or long-term ancillary reports; conference-only reports; retracted reports or unresolved integrity notices; non-English/no usable abstract; dates outside the window; prior exposure/duplicate families; unresolved eligibility/provenance. Do not exclude an eligible record for absent C, sparse annotations, difficult language, long spans, unstructured format, unfavorable results or model input limits. Missing registration or imperfect CONSORT reporting is not itself exclusion.

**7. Primary-result definition.** The earliest full journal report of the randomized comparison's prespecified principal health outcome(s), checked against the protocol/registry when available. If unregistered, require a clear primary-report description and documented screening agreement; do not infer primacy from significance. Of simultaneous eligible reports from the same trial family, choose earliest first-public report date, then lowest numeric PMID. Other reports are linked, not independent sampling units. [CONSORT 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11996237/) informs evidence fields, not a requirement that every eligible abstract comply perfectly.

**8. Duplicate detection.** Before sampling, compare candidate records with one another and all prior corpora. Exact identifier or normalized-text matches trigger documentary review. Near-match flags: normalized-title Levenshtein similarity >=.90; abstract word-5-gram Jaccard >=.80 or containment >=.90; titles with fewer than five tokens require corroboration. Also review shared trial acronyms/registry aliases and bibliographic links. These are fixed screening triggers, not proof that unflagged records are clean. Two screeners inspect flagged pairs and record decisions; unresolved suspected overlap is excluded before allocation. No learned embedding model is needed.

**9. Trial-family deduplication.** Build connected components from confirmed same-trial/overlapping-randomization evidence, not merely shared disease or investigators. Link companion reports, preprints, secondary analyses, follow-ups, corrections and shared participants. Conservatively group reports under a common master/platform registration unless separate randomization and nonoverlapping participants are documented. One canonical primary report per family in the entire acquisition. Any family represented in prior ACAD_PASS data is excluded. No family may cross qualification, DEV and EVAL.

**10. Identity/fingerprints.** Persist PMID, original/canonical DOI, original title, ordered abstract sections, publisher metadata, raw XML SHA256, canonical text SHA256 and normalized title/abstract SHA256. Canonical text is decoded PubMed text in source order, title followed by each abstract section with one LF separator; store explicit offsets and labels for each field. Preserve visible Unicode and punctuation; normalize neither case nor spelling for annotation offsets. Use zero-based half-open Unicode-code-point offsets. For matching only, use Unicode NFKC, casefolding and collapsed whitespace; lexical matching uses Unicode letter/digit runs and preserves numeric values. Record software/Unicode versions. Do not replace original source text with a normalized fingerprint representation.

**11. Registry matching.** Search PubMed accession fields, abstract, linked primary article and registry metadata for NCT, ISRCTN, ACTRN, ChiCTR, CTRI, IRCT, UMIN, jRCT, DRKS and EU/WHO-linked identifiers and aliases. Normalize case/separators; preserve source evidence. Cross-registration aliases must be linked. A missing ID requires manual trial-family checks using acronym, arms, enrollment dates, sites, population, sample size and author links. Missing ID does not establish independence. Unresolved suspected prior-family overlap means exclusion.

**12. Sampling.** Simple random sampling without replacement of eligible trial families; no domain quotas or score-based selection. The complete screened/deduplicated frame is frozen first. Assign the first 80 randomly ordered families to QUALIFICATION, next 400 to FRESH_DEV, next 5,000 to SEALED_FRESH_EVAL. All remaining records stay unused and inaccessible to developers. Fewer than 5,480 eligible families means STOP before PICO annotation and a new acquisition review; do not widen dates, lower sample size or fill from old corpora automatically.

**13. Randomization seed.** Before retrieval, the independent custodian generates one 256-bit random secret S and publishes SHA256(S) with a dated commitment. Rank families by HMAC-SHA256(S, UTF8("ACAD_PASS_FRESH2026_V1|allocation|" + canonical_family_id)), breaking ties lexically by family ID. Family ID is the lowest numeric PMID in the confirmed family component. No reseeding. Keep S and full membership manifests private until evaluation is consumed. Use domain-separated HMAC strings for audit ordering and the later statistical subsampling, never model output scores.

**14. FRESH_DEV size.** Exactly 400 trial families/documents. This is a bounded future development resource, not a guarantee of sufficient training data. No transfer from EVAL if development disappoints. Qualification documents never become training or evaluation data.

**15. SEALED_FRESH_EVAL size.** Exactly 5,000 trial families/documents; reject the proposed 400 as a strong four-class confirmation sample. This is a planning choice, not an empirically established optimum. At an illustrative C density .48/document, recall .20 and precision .95, 400 documents yield about 40 accepted C spans; 2,000 yield 202; 5,000 yield 505. At half that density, 5,000 yield about 253. Accepted independent families can be fewer than accepted spans. Therefore 5,000 improves support but does not guarantee power or a PASS. Resource approval must cover the full annotation workload. If unaffordable, stop and explicitly narrow the scientific objective under another protocol; do not silently call 400 sufficient.

**16. C-enriched challenge.** No additional C-enriched corpus in this acquisition (size 0). Preserve C by larger unbiased sampling and explicit support checks. A later challenge corpus could answer a conditional stress-test question under a separate plan, but cannot be pooled into headline precision/recall, repair EVAL support retrospectively or rescue a failed claim.

**17. P/I/C/O schema.** Version FRESH_PICO_TITLE_METHODS_V1: P denotes explicit enrolled/recruited participant descriptions; I denotes assigned experimental treatments/procedures; C denotes explicitly designated reference/control treatments, including placebo, sham, usual care or no treatment; O denotes measured endpoints/tests used as endpoints. A test assigned as treatment is I. Do not infer unmentioned entities or annotate stand-alone numerical results/statistical tests as O. Outside is a tagging state, not Outcome. Gold is annotated from the text, independently of any candidate bank.

**18. Boundaries.** Adopt the pinned [source manual](https://github.com/BIDS-Xu-Lab/section_specific_annotation_of_PICO/blob/bc4b878773192f38b2600ec830ca4208b82f7dc0/Annotation%20guidelines.pdf), with the explicit prospective resolutions below. Include attached articles. P uses the complete participant noun phrase, including attached descriptors, excluding introductory prepositions and explicit criteria lists. I/C retain preceding dose/form/frequency modifiers; exclude trailing dose/duration and following prepositional material; omit generic group labels. Separate coordinated treatments and abbreviations. O retains its endpoint noun phrase, directly attached statistical modifiers and one endpoint-defining prepositional attachment; exclude change/difference introductions, measured values, comparison clauses and extrinsic measurement times. Preserve intrinsic endpoint timing. Numeric prefixes inside endpoint names are not results. Trim only outside whitespace and delimiter punctuation, preserving internal punctuation. The manual's colored example marks “log serum level of PSA” rather than its change-introduction as the endpoint; text extraction alone loses this distinction.

Prospective resolution: when a postmodifier is required to complete a treatment name (e.g. a lexicalized “therapy of ...” term), follow the source restriction and record the omitted context in a note, rather than silently switching to clinical-concept boundaries. Composite endpoints retain their source-compatible summary mention and separately expressed component mentions, subject to the fixed flat projection below. For ellipsis, annotate the visible contiguous conjunct and store the shared-head link; never insert absent words or concatenate discontinuous text. No maximum token/span length in gold.

**19. Sections in scope.** Title + Methods, with P/I/C in titles and no title-O annotations. Full abstract is retained as role-disambiguation context. For structured abstracts, use PubMed NlmCategory=METHODS; where absent, map headers Methods, Materials and Methods, Patients and Methods, Design, Setting, Participants, Interventions and Main Outcome Measures to Methods; other headings are outside scope. For unstructured/mixed abstracts, two humans independently mark contiguous methods clauses (design, recruitment, allocation, interventions, prespecified measurements); a third adjudicates. Separate observed-results clauses from methods, without inventing text. No in-scope methods clause is a valid empty Methods input, not an exclusion.

The primary task is extraction on supplied, human-delimited Title+Methods, with the same full-abstract context available to the frozen future procedure. The supplied scope is an oracle section-selection condition. It cannot support an end-to-end full-abstract retrieval claim. Do not reuse a learned section filter to define gold or exclude records it misses.

**20. Repetition/nesting/overlap.** Every in-scope occurrence is distinct; retain title/method repetitions at distinct coordinates. Abbreviation and expansion are separate mentions. Store all independently justified source-schema spans in an immutable annotation layer. For the primary flat source-compatible target, deduplicate identical (document,start,end,class) entries, then greedily retain nonoverlapping spans sorted by decreasing character length, increasing start, then increasing end. Same-coordinate class conflicts must be resolved semantically before projection; never apply arbitrary class precedence. Suppressed nested/crossing spans remain in the immutable auxiliary layer and suppression counts are reported. This is an explicit prospective flat-task convention, not a claim that overlap does not exist. Never choose this projection using model success. Chunk splits cannot create new gold entities; valid BIO continuation remains one document-level entity, invalid initial-I is flagged.

**21. Multi-arm trials.** Label each stated tested arm I and each explicitly designated reference arm C. Role follows the occurrence's textual arm context, not the winning result, a drug dictionary or first mention. Multiple reference arms may all be C. A head-to-head trial without an expressed reference assigns both arms I and records NO_EXPLICIT_REFERENCE; do not manufacture C from ordering. An intervention applied to all arms with no contrastive role is I plus SHARED_BACKGROUND metadata; arm-specific repetitions follow their local roles. If one occurrence jointly refers to both tested and reference arms without separable mentions, use I plus SHARED_ROLE metadata. These explicit conventions preserve one class per occurrence; they limit the claim to textual-role PICO, not exhaustive trial-relationship extraction. Registry checks determine eligibility/family identity, not hidden labels absent from model-visible context.

**22. Annotators.** Two independent annotators for every document, each with a medical/allied-health qualification or graduate clinical epidemiology/evidence-synthesis training, demonstrated English RCT-reading competence and protocol qualification. A third senior clinical/evidence-synthesis adjudicator is required. A data-custodian role must be separate from model development; the adjudicator may hold it if access controls and audit duties are documented. Annotation staff seeing EVAL must not participate in later model design or tuning.

Use the 80 qualification documents as 40 training/discussion plus 40 blind qualification documents. The adjudicator fixes a reference independently before seeing the two qualification submissions. Require, for each annotator, exact typed-span macro F1 >=.85 and every class F1 >=.80, with at least 10 reference entities/class in the blind qualification portion. Insufficient qualification support or failed qualification means HOLD; no opportunistic extra examples or threshold relaxation. These are proposed operational qualification thresholds, not literature-proven universal standards.

**23. Adjudication.** Lock both submissions before comparison. The third expert first independently annotates a preselected 10% random audit of DEV/EVAL (40 and 500 documents), blind to both submissions, then reviews all disagreements and the complete text of every document, including jointly unannotated passages. Preserve omissions, boundary, role, section and overlap disagreements and the reason for each resolution. Do not resolve disagreements by majority character voting that creates a span nobody annotated. Unresolved semantic defects prevent gold freeze; no difficult-record deletion.

**24. Blinding.** Annotators are blind to split membership, model candidates, predicted classes/confidence, all B/J0/J1/R44C outputs and target performance. Each annotator is blind to the other until lock. The custodian alone holds split keys; developers have no candidate-frame access from which EVAL can be reconstructed. Annotation questions and adjudication examples involving EVAL remain within the annotation team.

**25. IAA.** Before adjudication report exact typed-span F1 (micro/macro/per class), exact boundary-only F1, type disagreement at equal coordinates, omission/addition counts, section-boundary agreement and adjudication rates. For overlap diagnostics use maximum one-to-one interval matching with character IoU >=.5; never call that exact agreement. Report both complete annotation and primary-projected layers. Optional token kappa uses a frozen deterministic tokenizer and explicit class/OUTSIDE coding; it is descriptive and cannot replace span F1. Main-corpus quality gate: pre-adjudication exact typed-span micro F1 >=.85 and each class >=.80, separately on DEV/EVAL, plus zero unresolved adjudication cases. The custodian releases only completion/quality-gate status before model freeze. Failure halts release, rather than removing hard documents.

**26. Versioning/immutability.** Hash raw text, scope map, manual/addendum, both independent submissions, adjudication log, full/projection gold, manifests and tool versions. Gold must be fully adjudicated and sealed before any DEV-driven model development. Corrections are append-only errata preserving original versions. Pre-evaluation corrections require an independent, model-blind review and a new gold commitment before prediction; after one-shot evaluation there is no replacement score or rewritten historical result. A substantive defect suspends claims and requires a separately reviewed successor benchmark, not silent rescoring.

**27. AI assistance.** For this protocol, no semantic AI prelabels, span suggestions, LLM adjudication or model-based section selection in either gold corpus. Mechanical offset/schema checks are allowed. AI-assisted labels can support a different, openly declared silver dataset; they count as independent human gold only if replaced by a complete fresh blind human annotation/adjudication process whose annotators never saw those suggestions, on unexposed records. Different model names or human approval of suggestions do not establish independence.

**31. Minimum gold support.** After annotation: FRESH_DEV requires >=50 primary gold entities/class from >=30 families; SEALED_FRESH_EVAL requires >=1,000 primary gold entities/class from >=500 families. **32. Minimum accepted predictions.** For a future confirmatory evaluation, each class must have >=200 accepted distinct spans across >=200 distinct trial families. Repeated mentions count in ordinary entity metrics, but cannot satisfy the independent-family requirement. Zero acceptance, missing outputs or nonfinite/malformed predictions cannot remove documents/gold denominators. Runtime failures remain explicit failures/abstentions with all gold retained; partial-population output cannot earn a success verdict.

**33. Precision criterion.** Preserve pooled exact-span per-class precision >=.90, macro precision >=.90 and per-class recall >=.20. Add a simultaneous confidence requirement for a clearly separate, trial-balanced precision estimand: select a trial uniformly among trials with at least one accepted class-c prediction, then select one of its accepted class-c spans uniformly. Require the lower bound for this correctness probability >=.90 for each class. This protects against repeated easy mentions dominating apparent confidence. It is not an exact confidence bound on pooled entity-weighted precision; do not claim otherwise. R44C gates/results are unchanged.

**34. Exact confidence method.** After predictions and their hashes are immutable, but before gold join, select exactly one accepted span/class/family using independent domain-separated HMAC ranks under S:
`ACAD_PASS_FRESH2026_V1|precision-audit|class|family_id|document_id|start|end`.
Select the lowest rank, with coordinate order as tie-breaker. Selection cannot use labels, correctness or confidence. For class c let n_c be contributing families and k_c exact-correct selected spans. Compute the one-sided Clopper–Pearson bound
`L_c = BetaQuantile(0.0125; k_c, n_c-k_c+1)`,
with L=0 if k=0. Require all four L_c >=.90; Bonferroni gives a 95% simultaneous model-based confidence statement under independent representative trial-family sampling. State that sampling/model assumption explicitly; dependence between unresolved trial families breaks it. Keep the numerical implementation/version frozen and test it on synthetic counts before any EVAL access.

For illustration, 95/100 gives L=.87723; 190/200 gives .90374; 475/500 gives .92358. With true trial-balanced precision .95, 200 independent contributing families have only about 58.3% probability of passing this class bound; 500 have about 98.0%. Thus the minimum support floor is not a power guarantee. These planning calculations use hypothetical Bernoulli counts, not new model predictions. No iid-entity Clopper–Pearson claim and no naive bootstrap substitute are authorized.

Any C-stratified descriptive result after the one-shot evaluation is secondary and cannot replace the full-population result.

## Statistical interpretation and implementation boundaries

The proposed 5,000-record evaluation is deliberately larger than the development corpus because the limiting requirement is reliable precision for the rare accepted C class, not merely collecting many gold spans. It is not known that the dated eligible frame contains 5,480 suitable independent families or that the annotation resources exist: neither was assumed or checked by collecting records.

The new confidence criterion is explicitly trial-balanced. The pooled entity precision remains a finite-sample point-estimate gate. If the project insists on a simultaneous population lower confidence bound for entity-weighted precision itself, this protocol explicitly rejects an iid span interval; a separately reviewed cluster-aware inferential plan would be needed before acquisition. Do not advertise the trial-balanced bound as proving that different estimand.

Benefits of these changes are a better supported C claim, honest units of independence, preserved construct identity and genuine separation of development from confirmation. Costs are a large human workload, a possible infeasible frame, and a conservative confirmation gate; even a valid model can produce an inconclusive result with sparse support. The overlap projection and textual-role conventions are explicit prospective additions and must not be called retroactive source-corpus corrections.
